# Navigation and contact

## Sub-features
Page structure and profile sources: [README.md](../../../../README.md#design-and-sources). The homepage is one long index: masthead (Janus's line portrait on the left, 200px wide, 160px at 1100px and below, drawing every stroke in at once on load; a vertical rule; then the name, contact links (Email, LinkedIn, GitHub, X, Resume) and section jump links on the right; the group is centered on the page, and at 560px and below the 120px portrait stacks above the centered text), then labelled sections and a quiet footer. Once the masthead scrolls away, a thin fixed bar takes its place, with the name (no avatar or portrait), section links (current one marked) and social links (hidden at 1100px and below; the section links scroll sideways when they do not fit). The masthead jump links are About, Building, Experience, Values, Books, Other interests; on phones they wrap. `/building/`, `/experience/`, `/about/` and `/bookshelf/` (the retired Bookshelf page) are redirect stubs to their home sections; error-page routing is documented in [README.md](../../../../README.md).

## User path and drive
Open `/`, use a jump link. `node verify.mjs` drives these paths in five browser contexts: it checks the section labels, the Email, GitHub and Resume destinations (the resume PDF must be served), the masthead mark (the line portrait `portrait.svg`, inlined and finished drawing with JavaScript, the image file without it, labelled "Line drawing of Janus Tsen"; no profile photo anywhere). From document startup, the motion cases record partly drawn strokes and increasing progress, then wait for all strokes to finish; reduced motion checks the fully drawn, unanimated portrait. The no-JavaScript case checks the loaded, visible SVG image. Returning to the top after scrolling must not replay the draw-in. Read `*-portrait-progress.json` for the recorded stroke samples and `*-portrait-3-finished.png` for the finished portrait. Photo checks allow only the known line-drawing images across the homepage and intro; the social-preview assets documented in [README.md](../../../../README.md#design-and-sources) are outside this rendered-page check. About must contain no media, and the bar no image or SVG. A slow-`site.js` pass checks that the loaded static image stays hidden until the swap and that the draw-in then starts from the beginning; the blocked-`site.js` intro case checks that the loaded portrait image still shows after the fallback. The main pass also checks a jump to Experience that lands below the bar, keyboard focus, and that each old page URL lands on its home section. Inspect footer destinations without sending email. LinkedIn may require sign-in.
