# Navigation and contact

## Sub-features
Page structure and profile sources: [README.md](../../../../README.md#design-and-sources). The homepage is one long index: masthead (Janus's line portrait, 200px wide, 160px at 1100px and below, drawing itself in once on load; then a vertical rule and the left-aligned name, contact links and section jump links; at 560px and below the 112px portrait sits above a horizontal rule), then labelled sections and a quiet footer. Once the masthead scrolls away, a thin fixed bar takes its place, with the name (no avatar or portrait), section links (current one marked) and social links (hidden at 1100px and below; the section links scroll sideways when they do not fit). The masthead jump links are About, Building, Experience, Thinking (Strategy and Values), Tech & AI, Design, Books and Other interests; on phones they wrap. `/building/`, `/experience/`, and `/about/` are redirect stubs to their home sections. The Bookshelf is the other content page; error-page routing is documented in [README.md](../../../../README.md).

## User path and drive
Open `/`, use a jump link, follow the Bookshelf link, then `← Janus Tsen` home. `node verify.mjs` drives these paths in five browser contexts: it checks the section labels, the Email and GitHub destinations, the masthead mark (the line portrait `portrait.svg`, inlined and finished drawing with JavaScript, the image file without it, labelled "Line drawing of Janus Tsen"; no profile photo anywhere). Before any scroll, the motion cases observe partly drawn strokes and increasing progress, then wait for all strokes to finish; reduced motion checks the fully drawn, unanimated portrait. The no-JavaScript case checks the loaded, visible SVG image. Returning to the top after scrolling must not replay the draw-in. Read `*-portrait-1-drawing.png`, `-2-progress.png` and `-3-finished.png`. Photo checks allow only the known line-drawing images across the homepage, intro and Bookshelf; the social-preview metadata and unused JPEG asset are outside the rendered page. About must contain no media, and the bar no image or SVG. A slow-`site.js` pass checks that the loaded static image stays hidden until the swap and that the draw-in then starts from the beginning; the blocked-`site.js` intro case checks that the loaded portrait image still shows after the fallback. The main pass also checks a jump to Experience that lands below the bar, keyboard focus, and that each old page URL lands on its home section. Inspect footer destinations without sending email. LinkedIn may require sign-in.
