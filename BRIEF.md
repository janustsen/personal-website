# Janus Tsen personal website

## Approved direction
The user asked for a broad-role professional portfolio showing their strongest work and AI fluency, with distinctive scroll motion. They chose founder/business judgment/product craft/AI execution; a kinetic studio; and a professional core with a small personal layer. Their refinement: "I want it to look cinematic too ... but the main focus should be ease of use and not stunningness of the website."

No Firstmate usage or interference. Work is isolated in this project. New copy is synthesized for the portfolio; no vault documents, client datasets, or private source artifacts are included.

## Design, authored within the delegated direction
- Vibe: clear, thoughtful, tactile, cinematic, approachable.
- Grammar: professional studio dossier. Persistent section links, one immediately readable identity, two featured work explanations, compact supporting work, AI method, experience, personal context, direct email contact.
- This grammar serves scan-and-explore hiring visits. It bans scroll hijacking, long pins, obligatory animation, hidden primary claims, fake product screenshots, and decorative loading screens. The filmic/continuous grammars impede scanning; live surface is wrong for a portfolio; poster sacrifices evidence; gallery limits context; chaptered editorial is too linear; cutlist is too busy; split stage invents an unnecessary comparison.
- Palette: frost #f7f9fa, silver-blue #eaf0f4, ink #182c3d, slate #526675, sapphire #2c57a4; dark chapter #193449. Manrope for all typography.
- Signature: three linked disciplines form one live evidence path as the visitor reaches the selected work. It links the visitor's initial positioning to concrete case studies.
- Hero layer contract: photographic glass sculpture is the distant plane; native identity and CTA remain fixed within normal document flow; footer is foreground. Image alone has restrained differential movement. Static silhouette remains complete.
- Distinct scenes, not a continuous flight. No video: the approved usability priority and a still with subtle depth supply the cinematic quality without loading or seeking costs.
- Assets: one original generated glass-sculpture still; native semantic diagrams explaining work, clearly not screenshots. No fabricated headshot or metrics.
- Mobile: independent hero crop below text, stacked projects, no horizontal rails. Reduced-motion and no-JavaScript content remain fully available.
